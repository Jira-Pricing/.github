## Starting a Jira Pricing Review

A structured pricing review begins with the team's goals and the work it needs to manage. Use a consistent evaluation process to compare requirements with current product offerings.

1. **Define Requirements:** List the project, issue tracking, reporting, and service management capabilities the team needs.
2. **Estimate Team Usage:** Determine the expected number of users and identify relevant access roles.
3. **Review Available Plans:** Compare current product options and document which features are included.
4. **Assess Integrations:** Identify documentation, collaboration, and service workflows that may affect product selection.
5. **Estimate Costs:** Review current subscription terms and calculate expected costs using the team's requirements.
6. **Record the Decision:** Document the selected option, relevant assumptions, and any capabilities that need further verification.

**Software safety note:** Use authorized accounts and official product information when reviewing subscriptions. Keep business records and configuration notes backed up, and confirm access permissions before sharing project or service data.
